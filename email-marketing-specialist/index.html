Email Marketing Specialist

Join Platinium Group in Monaco

Permanent role – Immediately available

Job Overview

We are looking for a Junior Marketing Specialist to support PG’s CRM and email marketing activities using Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC). You will contribute to the day-to-day execution of campaigns and customer journeys for PG brands and B2B clients, including audience management, A/B testing and performance monitoring.

Responsibilities

  • Build, test and deploy email campaigns in Salesforce Marketing Cloud.
  • Create and update customer journeys in Journey Builder.
  • Support acquisition, engagement and retention CRM campaigns.
  • Manage audiences, campaign QA, recurring automations and reporting.
  • Work with BI, marketing and sales teams on targeting and campaign activation.

Desired Skills & Experience

  • Around 1–2 years in email marketing, CRM or marketing automation.
  • Practical Salesforce Marketing Cloud experience and familiarity with Journey Builder.
  • Understanding of email best practices, customer segments and campaign metrics.
  • Strong attention to detail, organisation and proficient English.

Nice to have

Basic AMPscript or SQL knowledge, digital analytics experience, and exposure to B2B or multi-brand email campaigns.

Our Offer

An international, multidisciplinary environment in Monaco with training and support to deepen your Salesforce Marketing Cloud skills while working across different brands and campaign types.
