The Group

Who we are

Founded in 1985, Platinium Group SAM is one of the fastest-growing e-commerce companies in the international market, and is a key player within digital ticketing, marketing and logistics. Our cutting-edge and high-end ticketing technology is used by some of the most recognized sports events in the world. Combined with seamless on-site event logistics, it is praised as the most powerful box office software and comprehensive ticketing system in the industry.

Constantly thriving for evolution and pushing back boundaries, our approach to accessing, managing and interacting with data is the key to our customers’ success. A highly customer-centric approach and passion for simplicity, ease of use and effectiveness drive our Monaco-headquartered company’s global growth, including offices in six countries from Barcelona to Shanghai and serving clients worldwide.

Through Monte Carlo Travel and GPexperiences, our team comprehends long-time expertise in the luxury travel and Formula 1 hospitality business. With our consumer brand Gootickets.com, we also play a dominant role within the brokerage segment in the ticketing industry.
