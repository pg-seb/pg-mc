Monte Carlo Travel Agency, founded in 1985, is the travel agency that pioneered Platinium Group’s growing success and carried the company’s name on the global market. Thirty years later, the agency continues to provide world-class hospitality and travel with the same meticulous approach, taking traveling to a whole new dimension and building the roads to unforgettable memories by offering truly unique voyage experiences.
