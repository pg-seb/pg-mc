Platinium Group (PG) occupies a prominent role in the ticketing industry. Interacting with some of the biggest names of the Sports and Entertainment businesses, the company provides its powerful technology to international event organisers across the world. PG also operates on the B2C market via its own ticketing brand — Gootickets.com — and on the hospitality market through Monte-Carlo Travel.
