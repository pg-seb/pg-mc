Gootickets.com: Our Ticketing Portal

Our Ticketing Portal offers over 40 events across different sports to consumers around the World.

Gootickets.com is Platinium Group’s ticketing portal directly available to consumers around the world. Offering sports tickets which include all Formula 1 and MotoGP races, our brand also provides a wide range of tickets for Motocross, Tennis and other sporting activities such as Classic races, DTM and Golf.
